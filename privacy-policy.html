Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 25, 2026

Welcome to BallShift.

BallShift is a mobile puzzle game developed and published by Pixel Zero ("we", "our", or "us").

We respect the privacy of our players and are committed to protecting personal information, including information relating to children.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you play BallShift.

It also explains how advertising services, privacy choices, and applicable data protection rights are handled.

Our Commitment

BallShift is designed to be enjoyable for a broad audience, including children, teenagers, and adults.

We intend to apply additional privacy protections to children and players whose ages cannot be determined.

Advertising and related data processing will be enabled only when the applicable privacy and age-protection requirements have been met.

1. Information We Collect

BallShift is designed to provide puzzle gameplay without requiring players to create a personal account.

The game may process the following information.

1.1 Gameplay Information

  • Level progression and completion status.
  • Earned stars and achievements.
  • Unlocked game content.
  • In-game currency and rewards.
  • Game preferences and settings.

Game progress and preferences are primarily intended to be stored locally on the player's device.

Local gameplay saves are not automatically equivalent to information being uploaded to Pixel Zero's servers.

1.2 Device and Advertising Information

If advertising services are enabled for an eligible player, third-party advertising providers may process information such as:

  • Device type and model.
  • Operating system and version.
  • Application version.
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • Advertising identifiers, where permitted.
  • Advertising impressions and interactions.
  • Advertising performance and diagnostic information.
  • Privacy and consent signals.

The information actually processed depends on the enabled advertising networks, device configuration, applicable law, and player privacy choices.

We do not intend to collect precise GPS location through BallShift.

1.3 Age and Privacy Preferences

BallShift is intended to support age-appropriate privacy protections.

Age-screening information may be used to determine which advertising and privacy rules apply to a player.

Where possible, only the resulting age category will be retained locally rather than a complete date of birth.

Privacy preferences may also be stored or processed as needed to respect advertising consent decisions.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed through BallShift may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain gameplay functionality.
  • Save player progress and preferences.
  • Provide achievements and in-game rewards.
  • Deliver permitted advertisements.
  • Measure advertising performance.
  • Detect advertising fraud and technical abuse.
  • Maintain security and technical functionality.
  • Respect privacy and consent preferences.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

We do not intend to use children's personal information for behavioral advertising.

3. Advertising Services

BallShift is intended to support advertisements, including interstitial advertisements and optional rewarded advertisements.

Rewarded advertisements allow eligible players to voluntarily watch an advertisement in exchange for an in-game benefit.

Advertising availability and behavior may vary based on age, region, consent choices, and applicable platform requirements.

3.1 Unity LevelPlay

BallShift integrates Unity LevelPlay, an advertising mediation platform provided by Unity.

LevelPlay helps manage advertisements from participating advertising networks.

Depending on the enabled integrations and applicable permissions, Unity and participating providers may process device information, advertising events, technical identifiers, and related information.

For additional information, please review:

Unity Privacy Policy

3.2 Google AdMob

Google AdMob is planned as an advertising network within BallShift's Unity LevelPlay mediation configuration.

If Google AdMob is enabled in a released version of BallShift, Google may process device and advertising information according to its policies and applicable privacy settings.

Google's use of information may include advertisement delivery, measurement, security, and fraud prevention.

Where required, advertising personalization will depend on applicable privacy restrictions and consent choices.

3.3 Other Advertising Partners

BallShift may use additional advertising networks through Unity LevelPlay.

Only networks configured and enabled in the released version of the game will participate in advertisement delivery.

The applicable privacy practices depend on the advertising partners involved.

We will update this Privacy Policy when material changes are made to the advertising services used by BallShift.

4. Children's Privacy

BallShift is intended for a mixed audience, including children aged 6 and older, teenagers, and adults.

We recognize that children require additional privacy protection.

Where required by applicable law and platform policies, we intend to:

  • Use age-appropriate privacy protections.
  • Avoid behavioral or interest-based advertising to children.
  • Avoid transmitting advertising IDs and other restricted persistent identifiers from children or users of unknown age.
  • Restrict advertisements to appropriate content and formats.
  • Use only advertising SDKs and versions permitted for advertising to children.
  • Apply appropriate restrictions before initializing advertising services.
  • Avoid unnecessary collection of children's personal information.

If compliant advertising cannot be provided to a child or a player of unknown age, advertisements will not be served to that player.

Parents and legal guardians may contact Pixel Zero regarding questions or concerns about children's privacy.

5. Age Screening

BallShift is intended to use a neutral age-screening mechanism to determine which privacy protections apply.

The age-screening mechanism is intended to distinguish players who require child-specific protections from other players.

Where appropriate, the resulting age category may be stored locally.

Players whose ages are unknown will be treated conservatively for advertising and privacy purposes.

Age-screening information will not be used to encourage children to provide an older age.

6. Advertising Consent and GDPR

BallShift is intended to support privacy consent requirements applicable in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland, and other relevant regions.

Where required, an appropriate consent management platform may request consent before advertising-related processing occurs.

BallShift's advertising implementation includes a Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent integration.

Its production operation must be enabled and verified before advertising is released.

Where required by law, players will be able to provide, refuse, or withdraw consent.

Withdrawing consent will not affect the lawfulness of processing performed before withdrawal.

Where consent is legally required, we will not rely on consent that has not been validly obtained.

Other processing may be based on legitimate interests, contractual necessity, or legal obligations where permitted by applicable law.

7. Privacy Choices

BallShift is intended to provide accessible privacy controls for players where required.

These controls may include advertising consent preferences and other applicable privacy options.

The availability of individual controls may depend on a player's region, age category, and applicable law.

Players may also manage certain advertising preferences through their device settings.

Device-level advertising controls do not replace any privacy choices that BallShift is legally required to provide.
