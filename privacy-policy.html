Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 25, 2026

Welcome to BallShift.

BallShift is a mobile puzzle game developed and published by Pixel Zero.

We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how information is collected, used, and protected when you play our game.

This Privacy Policy explains how BallShift and its integrated third-party services may process information.

1. Information We Collect

BallShift is designed primarily as an offline-capable mobile puzzle game.

The game does not require players to provide a name or create an account to enjoy its core gameplay.

1.1 Gameplay Information

BallShift may store gameplay-related information locally on your device, including:

  • Completed levels
  • Unlocked levels
  • Earned stars
  • Achievements and rewards
  • Virtual in-game currency balances
  • Unlocked cosmetic items
  • Gameplay preferences and settings

This information is used to maintain your progress and provide the game's features.

1.2 Device and Advertising Information

When advertising services are enabled, third-party advertising partners may automatically collect or process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers
  • Device type and model
  • Operating system and version
  • IP address
  • Approximate geographic location inferred from network information
  • Advertising interactions
  • Ad impressions and clicks
  • App usage and diagnostic information

The specific information processed depends on the advertising SDKs, applicable privacy settings, consent choices, and device configuration.

2. Advertising Services

BallShift may display advertisements to support the development and maintenance of the game.

Advertisements may include:

  • Interstitial advertisements displayed between gameplay sessions or levels.
  • Optional rewarded advertisements that players may choose to watch in exchange for in-game benefits.

2.1 Google AdMob

BallShift uses or may use Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google.

Google and its advertising partners may process device information, advertising identifiers, and advertising interaction data to provide, measure, and improve advertisements.

Depending on applicable law and user choices, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

For more information:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google Uses Information

2.2 Unity LevelPlay

BallShift may use Unity LevelPlay as an advertising mediation platform.

If enabled, Unity LevelPlay can help deliver advertisements from participating advertising networks.

These advertising partners may process device identifiers, advertising-related information, and technical data in accordance with their respective privacy policies.
