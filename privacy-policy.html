Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 25, 2026

Welcome to BallShift.

BallShift is a mobile puzzle game developed and published by Pixel Zero ("we", "us", or "our").

We respect the privacy of our players, including children, teenagers, and adults.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, processed, stored, disclosed, and protected when you use BallShift.

It also explains our approach to advertising, children's privacy, parental inquiries, consent management, and applicable data protection rights.

Our Privacy Commitment

BallShift is intended for a broad audience, including children aged 6 and older.

We intend to apply additional privacy protections to children and players whose ages cannot be determined.

Advertising and related data processing will be enabled only when the applicable privacy, consent, and age-protection requirements have been satisfied.

1. Information We Collect

BallShift is designed to provide puzzle gameplay without requiring players to create an account.

The information processed by the game may include the following categories.

1.1 Gameplay Information

  • Level progression and completion status.
  • Earned stars and achievements.
  • Unlocked game content and cosmetic items.
  • In-game currency and rewards.
  • Gameplay preferences and settings.
  • Audio and other local game preferences.

Gameplay progress and preferences are primarily intended to be stored locally on the player's device.

Local gameplay saves are not automatically equivalent to information being transmitted to Pixel Zero servers.

1.2 Device and Advertising Information

If advertising services are enabled for an eligible player, third-party advertising providers may process information such as:

  • Device type and model.
  • Operating system and version.
  • Application version.
  • IP address.
  • Approximate geographic location derived from an IP address.
  • Advertising identifiers where legally permitted.
  • Advertising impressions and interactions.
  • Advertising performance information.
  • Diagnostic information.
  • Fraud prevention and security information.
  • Privacy and consent signals.

The information actually processed depends on the advertising SDKs included in the released application, the networks enabled, the player's age category, applicable laws, and privacy preferences.

We do not intend to collect precise GPS location information through BallShift.

1.3 Age and Privacy Preferences

BallShift is intended to support age-appropriate privacy protections.

Age-screening information may be used to determine which privacy and advertising restrictions apply to a player.

Where possible, the game will retain only an appropriate age category locally rather than storing a complete date of birth.

Privacy preferences and consent signals may also be stored or processed to respect applicable privacy choices.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed through BallShift may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain gameplay functionality.
  • Save game progress and player preferences.
  • Provide achievements and in-game rewards.
  • Deliver permitted advertisements.
  • Measure advertising performance.
  • Detect advertising fraud and abuse.
  • Maintain application security.
  • Diagnose technical problems.
  • Respect player privacy preferences.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not intend to use children's personal information for behavioral or interest-based advertising.

3. Advertising Services

BallShift is intended to support advertising, including interstitial advertisements and optional rewarded advertisements.

Rewarded advertisements allow eligible players to voluntarily view an advertisement in exchange for an in-game benefit.

Advertisement availability and behavior may differ depending on the player's age, location, consent choices, and applicable platform requirements.

3.1 Unity LevelPlay

BallShift integrates Unity LevelPlay, an advertising mediation platform provided by Unity.

Unity LevelPlay manages advertising delivery through participating advertising networks.

Depending on enabled integrations and applicable permissions, Unity and its advertising partners may process device information, technical identifiers, advertising events, and related information.

Learn more: Unity Privacy Policy

3.2 Unity Ads

Unity Ads is included in the advertising integration identified during the project's development audit.

If active in a released version, Unity Ads may process information needed to deliver, measure, secure, and manage advertisements, subject to applicable privacy requirements.

Its applicable data practices are described in Unity's privacy documentation.

3.3 Google AdMob

Google AdMob is planned as an advertising network within BallShift's Unity LevelPlay mediation configuration.

If Google AdMob is enabled in a released version of BallShift, Google may process device and advertising information in accordance with applicable privacy settings and Google's policies.

This may include information used for advertisement delivery, measurement, security, and fraud prevention.

Advertising personalization will be restricted according to applicable laws, player age, and privacy choices.

Learn more:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google Uses Information

3.4 Other Advertising Partners

BallShift may use additional advertising networks through Unity LevelPlay.

Only networks configured and enabled in a released version of the game are intended to participate in advertisement delivery.

We will update this policy when material changes are made to our advertising services or data practices.

4. Children's Privacy

BallShift is intended for a mixed audience, including children aged 6 and older, teenagers, and adults.

We recognize that children require additional protection under laws and platform requirements, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) where applicable.

Our intended approach to protecting children includes:

  • Applying appropriate age-based privacy restrictions.
  • Not serving personalized or behavioral advertisements to children.
  • Avoiding collection or transmission of advertising IDs and other restricted persistent identifiers from children.
  • Restricting advertisements to child-appropriate content and formats.
  • Using advertising SDKs and versions permitted for advertising to children under applicable platform policies.
  • Applying privacy restrictions before initializing advertising services.
  • Avoiding unnecessary collection of children's personal information.
  • Treating players whose ages cannot be established conservatively.

Where required, we will obtain appropriate parental consent before collecting or using children's personal information for purposes that require such consent.

If compliant advertising cannot be provided to a child or a player of unknown age, advertisements will not be served to that player.

Parents and legal guardians may contact Pixel Zero to raise questions or concerns regarding children's personal information.

If we become aware that children's personal information has been collected or processed in violation of applicable requirements, we will take appropriate corrective action, including deletion where required.

5. Age Screening

BallShift is intended to use a neutral age-screening mechanism to determine which privacy protections apply.

The age-screening process is intended to distinguish players who require child-specific protections from other players.

Where appropriate, the resulting age category may be stored locally.

Age-screening information will not be used to encourage children to misrepresent their ages.

Players whose ages are unknown will receive conservative advertising and privacy treatment.

Any future changes to the age-screening implementation will be evaluated against applicable children's privacy requirements.

6. Advertising Consent and GDPR

BallShift is intended to support applicable privacy consent requirements in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, Switzerland, and other relevant jurisdictions.

Where required, an appropriate consent management platform will be used to request consent before advertising-related processing takes place.

BallShift's development configuration includes an integration with Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP).

Production operation of this consent integration must be enabled and verified before advertising is released.

Where applicable, eligible players will be able to:

  • Review information about advertising data use.
  • Provide consent.
  • Refuse consent.
  • Change or withdraw consent.
  • Access applicable privacy choices.

Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing performed before withdrawal.

Where consent is legally required, we will not rely on consent that has not been validly obtained.

Depending on the activity and applicable law, other processing may rely on contractual necessity, legitimate interests, or compliance with legal obligations.

These legal bases will be evaluated according to the specific processing activity and applicable jurisdiction.

7. Privacy Choices

BallShift is intended to provide accessible privacy controls where required by applicable law.

These controls may include advertising consent preferences and other applicable privacy options.

The game is intended to make supported privacy options available through its Settings interface.

The availability of specific privacy controls may depend on the player's region, age category, and applicable law.

Players may also manage certain advertising preferences through their device settings.

Device-level advertising controls do not replace any privacy controls that BallShift is legally required to provide.

Until the production privacy controls have been implemented and verified, advertising that requires those controls will remain disabled.

8. United States Privacy Rights

Certain United States privacy laws, including applicable state privacy laws, may provide eligible residents with rights relating to their personal information.

Depending on the applicable law, these rights may include:

  • The right to know whether personal information is being processed.
  • The right to access certain personal information.
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • The right to request deletion of personal information.
  • The right to receive a portable copy of certain personal information.
  • The right to opt out of certain forms of targeted advertising.
  • The right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, where applicable.
  • The right to limit certain uses or disclosures of sensitive personal information, where applicable.
  • The right not to be unlawfully discriminated against for exercising applicable privacy rights.

Advertising-related data sharing may qualify as a sale, sharing, or targeted advertising under certain state privacy laws, depending on the processing involved.

We intend to provide legally required opt-out mechanisms before enabling advertising practices that require them.

Where applicable, we will honor recognized privacy requests in accordance with the law.

Players and authorized representatives may contact Pixel Zero using the contact information provided below to submit applicable privacy requests.

We may need to verify the requester's identity or authority before fulfilling certain requests.

We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

9. Data Sharing and Third Parties

BallShift may share or permit access to certain information through third-party services that are enabled in the game.

These may include:

  • Unity LevelPlay.
  • Unity Ads.
  • Google AdMob, if enabled.
  • Other advertising networks enabled in the released application.
  • Technical service providers needed for permitted application operations.

Such providers may process information for advertisement delivery, measurement, security, fraud prevention, and other disclosed purposes.

Third-party providers may maintain their own privacy policies and data retention practices.

We encourage players and parents to review the privacy policies of the relevant providers.

This statement does not mean that advertising-related transfers can never constitute a sale or sharing under applicable privacy laws.

10. Data Storage and Retention

BallShift is designed to store gameplay progress and preferences primarily on the player's device.

This may include level progress, achievements, rewards, unlocked content, and settings.

Local gameplay information may remain on the device until it is deleted, reset, or otherwise removed.

Uninstalling BallShift may remove local game information, depending on operating system backup and restoration behavior.

10.2 Advertising and Consent Data

If advertising services are enabled, third-party providers may retain permitted advertising, diagnostic, and consent-related information.

Retention periods may vary according to the provider, the type of data, applicable law, and the purpose for which the information is processed.

Details about third-party retention practices are available in the relevant providers' privacy policies.

10.3 Privacy Requests

Information provided in connection with a privacy request may be retained for the period reasonably necessary to respond to the request, document compliance, resolve disputes, or meet applicable legal obligations.

We will not retain personal information longer than necessary for the relevant purpose, subject to applicable legal requirements.
